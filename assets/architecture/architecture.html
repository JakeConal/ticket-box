TicketBox Architecture

End-to-end concert ticketing — audience, organizer, and gate-checker surfaces on a Spring Boot + Next.js platform

Client Surfaces

  • • Audience web — discovery, queue, checkout, QR e-tickets
  • • Organizer admin — concerts, checkers, VIP guests, refunds
  • • Checker app — offline-first QR scan and VIP admission
  • • Local SQLite + SecureStore caches assignments and keys

Platform Services

  • • JWT authentication and role-based authorization
  • • Redis-backed sale queues with admission windows
  • • Idempotent purchase, payment, and refund handling
  • • Signed QR issuance and cross-device check-in sync

Data & Integrations

  • • PostgreSQL 17 with Flyway migrations
  • • Redis 8 for queues and rate limiting
  • • Mailpit SMTP for transactional email
  • • VNPay / MoMo sandboxes, NVIDIA artist bios
